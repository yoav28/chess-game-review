Three-check, King of the Hill, Atomic, Horde, Crazyhouse, and Giveaway analysis use the unmodified fairy-stockfish-nnue.wasm 1.1.12 distribution.
The application selects UCI_Variant=3check, kingofthehill, atomic, horde, crazyhouse, or antichess (Giveaway), follows the selected thread count, sets Hash=32, and disables NNUE. Evaluation is classical; no separate NNUE weights are supplied or fetched.
